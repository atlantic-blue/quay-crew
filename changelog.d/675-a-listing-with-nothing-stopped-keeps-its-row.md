**The reason column arrives with a stopped row and goes away with it.** `krewe job list` says why a
job stopped or failed, in a column beside the outcome. Most listings hold neither kind of row. A
column that is on every listing is a column of blanks on most of them. A person stops reading a
column of blanks by the second week.

So the column is there when a row of that listing carries a reason. It is absent otherwise. A listing
where nothing stopped and nothing failed prints the row it always printed: the identifier, the phase,
the stage, the outcome, then the title. No cell is added to that row. No run of spaces is added
between the outcome and the title. The listing that reads every project keeps its address column in
the same place.

A pending job the system holds back for room carries a reason too, and that one is not on the row. It
is one fact about the machine rather than one fact per row, so the listing says it once underneath as
`held: ...`. With a stopped row beside it the column exists, and the held row's cell in it stays
empty.
