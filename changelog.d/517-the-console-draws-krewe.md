**The console's wordmark spells KREWE.** It spelled QUAY, which is the product's first word beside a
command that spells the other half of the name. The command a person types is `krewe`, and the mark
now says it.

Same idiom and same cost in rows. Three rows of block letters, and each row carries two rows of the
letters through the half block characters. So the header still costs three rows, and the mark is
still the tallest thing in it.

**Five letters are seven columns wider than four.** The mark is 43 columns and it was 36. Beside a
header carrying the build and the address, it is drawn from 80 console columns up, where it was drawn
from 73. Both numbers are measured against this tree.

Under that width the mark is not drawn at all. That is the behaviour it always had. The header keeps
the build and the way to help, and the mark gives way rather than being drawn over the top of them. A
conversation opened beside the console leaves it 84 columns. That is the case that matters, and it is
four columns clear.
