**The console view that lists what a session ran is called `exec`.** It was called `tasks`, which was
the fourth word in the console for something the command line, the store and the wire each call a
task. The view does not list what a session was asked to do next; it lists what it has already run.

Every word that opened it opens it now: `tasks`, `task`, `history` and `h` all reach it, beside
`exec` and `e`. A word that quietly stops working is how an operator learns to distrust the rest of
the command bar, so none of them was dropped.

`t` on a session still opens it. The key was named after the word that moved, and a key lives in
fingers, so it stayed where it was.

Nothing outside the console moved. `krewe task`, the `tasks` table, the `ListTasks` call and the
`<workspace>.tasks` topic are what they were.
