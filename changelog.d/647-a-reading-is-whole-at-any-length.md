**A job says what it understood at whatever length the work needs.** The stage in front of the plan
asked a session for a reading and then refused it for its length. Job a3d72b11 wrote a correct
reading of 859 bytes against a ceiling of 600. It said which of its findings a person told it and
which it filled in itself. The system refused the whole reply, asked once more, and stopped the job.
Ten million tokens went to that job. Nobody read a word of the reading.

The reader now keeps the text. `UnderstandingLimit`, `IdeationLineLimit` and `IdeationLimit` stay as
the guides a session is asked to write to, and none of them refuses a reply. A reading over one of
them lands whole on the row, reaches the person who asked for it, and the job waits for that person
instead of stopping. It costs the one task the stage always cost, because a long reading is no longer
asked for a second time.

The length of a reading belongs to the person who reads it.
