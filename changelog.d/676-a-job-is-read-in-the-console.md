**A person reads a whole job in the console.** A row of the jobs listing is nine cells. The brief a
person wrote, the questions the job asked with the answers it got, the plan and whether anybody
approved it, the record of what each vertical became with the picture it was shown with, and the
answer at the end were all somewhere else, so a reader left the console and ran `krewe job show`.

Enter on a job row opens that job now, and the page carries every one of those fields whole. Nothing
is cut to the width of a cell: a brief, an answer and a build record are read in the panel the
console already reads a task in, and `j` and `k` scroll it. Escape closes it.

Enter used to descend into what the job's session ran, which is one level past the row a person
pointed at. That is on `w` now. A part of a job is a run rather than a job, so enter on one still
goes down into what that run did.
